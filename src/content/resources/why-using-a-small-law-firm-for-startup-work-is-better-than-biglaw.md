<p>Why Using a Small Law Firm for Startup Work is Better Than Biglaw</p>

<p>Starting a new business can be an exciting and challenging experience, and it's important to have the right support and resources in place to help you succeed. This is even moreso the case in the startup world, which face an always-changing landscape, venture capital investors, and a healthy amount of politicking. While many entrepreneurs turn to biglaw firms for legal assistance, there are compelling reasons to consider using a small law firm instead. Here’s why:</p>

<p>Smaller Firms are Cheaper</p>

<p>One of the biggest advantages of working with a small law firm is that they tend to be more cost-effective than biglaw firms. Smaller firms have lower overhead costs, which means they can charge lower fees for their services. This can be a huge advantage for startups, which often have limited budgets and need to be mindful of their expenses. Such is the case for our firm, which pays for website hosting costs, Docusign, and filing fees, but little else.</p>

<p>Smaller Firms are More Responsive</p>

<p>Another advantage of working with a small law firm is that they tend to be more responsive and accessible than biglaw firms. Smaller firms have fewer clients, which means they can provide more personalized attention to each client. This can be especially important for startups, which may need quick and effective legal assistance to address any number of issues. My clients can give me a call anytime and I call them back ASAP.</p>

<p>Smaller Firms are More Invested</p>

<p>Finally, smaller law firms are often more invested in the success of their clients than biglaw firms. Because they have fewer clients, small law firms have a greater stake in the success of each individual client. Some Biglaw firms are so busy that client emails are left on unread for weeks at a time. Smaller firms, on the other hand, have bandwidth to prioritize their startup clients.</p>

<p>Starting a new business is a big step, and it's important to have the right legal support in place. While biglaw firms may have the resources and experience to handle complex legal issues, they can also be overly expensive and unresponsive. In contrast, small law firms are often cheaper, more responsive, and more invested in the success of their clients. For startups looking for high-quality legal support at an affordable price, a small law firm may be the best choice.</p>
