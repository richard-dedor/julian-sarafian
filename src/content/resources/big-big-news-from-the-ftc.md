<p>This is a big change from the previous guidance, which only required that the post, in general, have a conspicuous disclosure.</p>

<p>This means that placing #ad in the caption <strong>is not sufficient</strong> to abide by Federal advertising laws. Instead, you must place a disclosure (like #ad) in the caption AND disclose in the video that the post is sponsored. You can do this by, e.g.</p>

<ul><li>Stating “thanks to Apple for sponsoring this video” or “this video is sponsored by Apple”</li><li>Adding “#ad” as a text block that rolls in an obvious spot of the video for at least 10 seconds</li></ul>

<p>If you don’t do this, <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/ftc-influencer-warning-disclosures-artificial-sweetener-promos-rcna125396">you are opening yourself up to liability from the FTC, with fines up to $50,000 per violation</a>.</p>
