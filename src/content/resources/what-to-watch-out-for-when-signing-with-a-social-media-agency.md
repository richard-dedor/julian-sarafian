<p>Social media agencies can provide tremendous value to creators by finding them deals and helping them build their brands. But they can also be predatory, locking creators into multi year terms, taking more than their fair share of commissions, and misappropriating creators' name image and likeness. </p>

<p>In this video, Julian breaks down what you as a creator should look for when signing with an agency. Hint: he thinks you should really hire an attorney like himself to help you negotiate these agreements!</p>
